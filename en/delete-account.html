Delete your Ecleptic account

Ecleptic app — publisher: Auguste Phily-Priou

You can delete your account and all your data at any time, free of charge, from the app or by email, without having to reinstall it.

1. From the app (immediate)

Open Ecleptic, then Settings › Account › Delete my account and confirm. Your account and your data are erased right away.

2. Without the app, by email

Write to us at contact@ecleptic.app from your account's address, with the subject "Delete my Ecleptic account". If you signed up with Apple and hid your email, give the account's first name and its approximate sign-up date. We delete your account and your data within 30 days at most and confirm it by email.

Before deleting: your subscription

Deleting your account does not stop an active Zenith subscription: it is managed by your store. Cancel it first in your App Store account (Settings › your name › Subscriptions) or on Google Play (Play Store › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).

What is deleted

  • your account (email, first name, profile, goals, preferences);
  • all your tracking data: sleep, meals and meal photos, workouts, measurements, weight, body scans, scores, biological age, conversations with Eos;
  • your private food entries, corrections and reports.

Backup copies are purged within 30 days.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Billing data: 10 years (accounting obligation), with restricted access;
  • Technical and security logs: 12 months at most;
  • Usage statistics attached to an installation identifier unrelated to the account: 13 months at most; aggregated counts without identifier: 25 months;
  • Nutritional values you had approved and published in the shared food database: they stay, with no link to you.

Data stored in your phone's health app (Apple Health, Health Connect) is not affected: it belongs to you and stays on your phone. Details are in the privacy policy, article 6.
