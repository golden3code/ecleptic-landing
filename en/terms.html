The User's profile, photos and videos immediately cease to be visible and the User leaves their teams and their coach; their ranking lines disappear at the nightly update; their partners and the participants in their ongoing challenges then see only their nickname, marked inactive. If the Society is not turned back on within 30 days, the User's Society-specific data is erased. The User's workouts and other data remain in the Account.

Activation constitutes explicit consent to the sharing, with the people chosen, of the designated sports data (article 16 and the Privacy Policy). Each type of data has its own setting (Settings › Social network): "No one", "Partners" or "Everyone", the latter meaning Society members. By default, the profile, photo, workouts, records and places are visible to partners only; weight and age categories are visible to no one.

The refusal is immediate and automatic (word filter, with no human involvement); it is reasoned, and the User may choose another nickname or contest the decision. A validated nickname may be changed at most once every 14 days.

  • Profile photo: optional. It must show the User or a neutral image, never a third party without their agreement.
  • Anonymous mode: the User may appear in rankings without their nickname.

The User warrants holding the necessary rights, in particular the image rights of the people shown. The User grants the Publisher, free of charge, on a non-exclusive basis and only for as long as they remain online, the right to host them, to adapt them technically (cropping, compression) and to display them to authorised persons. Ecleptic tolerates no objectionable content and no abusive behaviour. The profile photo is not filtered by artificial intelligence: it is checked after a report. A report hides it immediately until a moderator decides. Three confirmed removals deprive the User of a photo for 90 days.

9 bis.4. What is never published

The GPS track of a workout, real-time location, heart rate, exact weight, exact age and health data other than sports data are never shown to other users. Rankings are updated by the day, with no time of day shown.

A partnership requires the agreement of both people. A request may be refused without explanation. Challenges, Expeditions and the league are games between members, with no financial stake or prize. 9 bis.7. Places, spots and performances

  • Places and spots: gyms, pools, stadiums and other facilities come from open data (Overture Maps Foundation); zones and spots come from IGN outlines or from member suggestions accepted by the Ecleptic team. Their presence in the Application does not imply any partnership with the operator of the place. The place of a workout is attached by Ecleptic, from the position at the end of the workout, a check-in or the place's QR code.
  • Performances: a performance outside plausible limits (world records, abnormally fast progression) is kept in the personal history but excluded from rankings until verified. Distinctions (crown, "Legend of the place", etc.) require a sufficient level of proof (recording by a watch, video or witness depending on the sport) and may be withdrawn in case of well-founded doubt.
  • Proof videos: a video is seen only by the place's reviewers (Ecleptic team, approved manager, Founders who are still members). A reviewer decides in good conscience, without being an official referee. The reviewer undertakes to watch the video for this decision alone, not to copy or record it, not to show it to others and not to say anything about it outside their decision; any breach removes this role from the reviewer and falls under article 9 bis.14. A video is deleted 30 days after the decision.

9 bis.18. Teams

A member may create a team or join one with its code. An account belongs to only one team at a time, and a team has 12 members at most. Members see the team's name, as well as each member's nickname, avatar and number of workouts for the week, never the content of those workouts. A workout hidden from the profile does not count.

The administrator (the person who creates the team, then the member who joined earliest among those who remain) chooses the name and the goal, renews the code and may remove a member. A team's name is content published under the responsibility of its administrator: it follows the same rules as the nickname (9 bis.2), a member may report it, and moderation may remove the team. Leaving a team or turning off the Society removes the member immediately; the team disappears with its last member. A team is a game between members, with no financial stake or prize.

9 bis.19. Coaches

A coach is an adult member whose account has been validated by the Ecleptic team. An adult may link to one coach only at a time, using the coach's code, after a separate agreement, unticked by default, of which the Publisher keeps proof (timestamp, version). The coach then sees all of that student's workouts, including those hidden from the public profile: date, type, duration, distance, effort and title. The coach never sees sleep, nutrition, weight, heart rate, tracks or places.

The student may withdraw this agreement at any time, in Team: access stops immediately. Blocking the coach, turning off the Society or the withdrawal of coach status also end it.

The coach undertakes to use these workouts only to support the student, not to distribute them, and to comply with the rules applicable to their activity, in particular those of the French Sports Code if they teach for remuneration. Before any validation, the coach accepts the coach's commitment and, if they teach for remuneration, provides their professional sports educator card (carte professionnelle d'éducateur sportif). Validation of a coach account guarantees neither the coach's qualifications nor their advice. The Publisher is not a party to any relationship between a coach and a student, and is not involved in any payment between them.

9 bis.20. Messaging

Messaging lets members write to their partners, their team and their Expedition. It is reserved for members whose Society is active. A member may write to an individual person only if the two are partners.

A private conversation between an adult and a minor is refused, even between partners.

Messages are text, or a workout card that the User chooses to share. Each message passes, on sending, through an automatic filter of forbidden words, the same one as for nicknames: a refused message is not sent and its author is informed immediately. This filter keeps nothing and produces no decision on the account.

The User may reply to a message, which then appears quoted. The User may edit the text of their message for 24 hours after sending it, unless it accompanies a workout card or the conversation is closed. The new text passes through the same filter and the message carries the mention "edited". The earlier version is not kept, except for the copy attached to a report already sent.

The Ecleptic team reads a message only if a member of the conversation reports it: a copy of the message then accompanies the report. The Publisher may remove that message and take the measures provided for in article 9 bis.14, with a reasoned decision (9 bis.13) that can be contested (9 bis.15).

The rules of conduct (9 bis.5) apply to messages. The User may block a member (9 bis.16), which hides that member's messages and ends their private conversation, and may turn off notifications for each conversation.

A new member of a team or an Expedition does not see the messages written before their arrival. A member who leaves no longer has access to them, and that member's messages remain visible to the other members, unless the member's Account is deleted. Writing in an Expedition closes 7 days after its end.
