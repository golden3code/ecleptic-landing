Gyms and partner places

Your gym,
its board.

Ecleptic ranks your members' consistency, place by place: who shows up most this month, who is improving, who opened the gym's board. The kit below gives you what you need to display it at your place. It's free.

What your gym gets

  • The gym's board. Consistency (active days over 90 days), Progress and Peaks per lift, opening at 5 active members.
  • Seasons. On the 1st of each month, the previous month's top 10 are frozen; the hall of fame keeps the best seasons.
  • Founding members. The gym's first 10 active members keep that title for good.
  • The City Cup. Every month, your gym against the other gyms in your city.
  • A screen for your TV and a QR poster for the front desk.

In three steps

  1. Become the place manager. Install Ecleptic, open your gym's page and tap “Do you manage this place?”. We check (a call or an email), then turn on your access.
  2. Show the screen. On the place page, “Gym screen” gives you a link. Open it on the screen of your choice (TV browser, computer, streaming stick): it updates itself every 15 minutes.
  3. Put up the poster. From the place page, print the QR poster and place it at the front desk. A scan checks your members in and adds them to the board.

Privacy

  • Only members who agreed in the app appear on screen, and only by their username: no photo, name, time or route.
  • Minors never appear. Anyone can withdraw their consent at any time.
  • The screen link is protected by a key you can change in the app if the link has been shared around.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. The kit, the screen and the poster are free.
