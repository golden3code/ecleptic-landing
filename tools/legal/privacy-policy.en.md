# Privacy policy — Ecleptic

**Last updated: 23 November 2026**

This policy describes how the **Ecleptic** application (the "Application") processes its users' personal data, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the amended French Data Protection Act no. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 ("loi Informatique et Libertés").

## Article 1 — Data controller

- **Data controller**: Auguste Phily-Priou, **sole trader (Entrepreneur individuel, EI)**
- **Address**: 10 Rue du Commerce, 86400 Civray, France
- **SIREN**: 132 170 234 R.C.S. Poitiers
- **Privacy contact**: contact@ecleptic.app
- **Data protection officer (DPO)**: not appointed to date.

## Article 2 — Data collected

### 2.1. Account and identification data

When the account is created and during the welcome questionnaire: email address (or the identifier provided by "Sign in with Apple" / Google, on iPhone as on Android), first name or nickname, date of birth, sex, height, goals and tracking preferences.

### 2.2. ⚕️ Health and wellness data (special category — art. 9 GDPR)

The Application processes data that belongs to the **special categories of data** within the meaning of article 9 GDPR (data concerning health). It is clearly identified and processed with enhanced safeguards:

- physical activity data (workouts, heart rate, estimated VO2 max, energy expenditure);
- sleep data (duration, stages, awakenings, how you felt);
- nutrition data (meals, meal photos, estimated intake, photos of nutrition labels, food entries and corrections — see 2.6);
- body data (weight, measurements, estimated body composition, body scan photos);
- derived indicators (scores, "biological age" estimate, recommendations);
- physiological situation: what the user declares in "My situation" or at sign-up, and what the Application infers from data already collected (see 2.13);
- data imported from the system's health services (Apple Health on iPhone, Health Connect on Android) or from connected devices, with the user's consent (see 2.7);
- on Android, during a workout and at the user's request: heart rate and beat-to-beat intervals read live from a **Bluetooth** heart rate sensor (chest strap, watch in broadcast mode). Bluetooth is only used to connect to the chosen sensor: it is never used to locate the user;
- conversations with Eos, the Application's AI assistant (free-text messages, which may contain health information);
- approximate location (for weather and altitude), precise location during a GPS-tracked workout and, at the end of a session recorded live, the location at that moment to recognise the place of the session (see 2.8), if the user allows it.

### 2.3. Payment data

Subscriptions are purchased in-app through the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android): payment is handled by Apple or by Google, and the publisher **never** receives card details. Through its subscription management provider (RevenueCat), the publisher only receives information about the subscription status (plan purchased, start and expiry dates, renewal status).

**A coach's subscription.** A coach can also take out the coach space subscription on the website, through a **Stripe** payment page: the card is never entered at Ecleptic. Ecleptic receives from Stripe the tier, the status, the dates, the customer and subscription identifiers, and the amounts paid or refunded, never the card number.

**Purchases from a coach.** A coach can sell their sessions, packages and classes, in the Application or on their public page. Payment is made on a Stripe page opened in the coach's name, under the coach's own contract with Stripe: the card is never entered in Ecleptic and the price goes to the coach. Ecleptic receives from Stripe the amount, the date and the references of the payment and of its refunds, never the card number (see 2.14).

### 2.4. Technical and usage data

Technical account identifier, sign-in timestamps and server-side technical logs (security, usage quotas for AI features, errors and processing times). **Usage statistics**: the Application records usage events (screen shown, onboarding step completed, feature used, error encountered), attached to a **random installation identifier**, unrelated to the account, renewed at every sign-out and at least every 13 months. These events **never** contain health data (no weight, heart rate, calories, score, biological age or questionnaire answer) or any text you typed (food search, message to Eos, meal title). They only carry categories (for example "meal logged by photo") and technical information (app and system version — iOS or Android —, platform, device model, language, device region). The IP address is not stored: to prevent abusive submissions, only a salted fingerprint of the address is used to count the submissions of the hour, with no link to the events, and it is deleted after 2 hours at most. No advertising tool is built in.

**Last day opened**: for each account, Ecleptic keeps the last day on which the Application or the coaches' website was opened, the platform (iPhone, Android or website) and the version of the Application. It is a single line per account, replaced at most once a day, with no health data. It is used to count active accounts and to know whether a coach still uses their space. It never triggers a reminder: a reminder email is only sent to people who have agreed to it.

**A coach's origin**: when a user signs up as a coach, Ecleptic keeps the code of the link through which they arrived (for example that of a partner school or trainer), the name of that link's campaign and their optional answer to "Who sent you?". This information is used to know which channels bring coaches and, where applicable, to pay the partner who recommended them. It can no longer be changed two days after the application is sent.

### 2.5. Mandatory or optional data

The data needed to create the account and provide the service is marked as mandatory; without it, the service concerned cannot be provided. Other data (in particular most health data) is **optional**: the user chooses which tracking features to turn on.

### 2.6. Contributions to the food database

When a food cannot be found or an entry looks wrong, the user may, if they wish:

- **photograph a product's nutrition label**: the photo is read by an AI model (see article 4), which extracts the values; the user checks and corrects them before saving;
- **create a food entry** from these values (name, brand, barcode, nutritional values, serving);
- **correct** an existing entry or **report an error** (reason and optional note);
- **correct the match** of a food recognised when a meal is analysed.

A created entry stays **private**: it only serves its author. The publisher only reviews it if the user **submits it for review**; the label photo is then kept only for that check. A correction or an entry approved at review is then published in the shared food database **with no link to the account** (no name, nickname or identifier): only the product's nutritional values are published, never the photo. Reports are only used for review and are never published.

These contributions relate to what the user eats: they receive the same treatment as nutrition data (2.2).

### 2.7. Data read from Health Connect (Android) and Apple Health (iPhone) — limited use

**What is read**, only for the types the user allows on the Health Connect (or Apple Health) screen: sleep and its stages; workouts (type, duration, distance, calories); heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability; VO2 max; oxygen saturation; respiratory rate; skin temperature (on Android, the deviation from the baseline); active energy and resting energy; steps and distance; running, cycling or rowing power, cadence and speed; periods; **blood pressure and blood glucose** (Apple Health and Health Connect); **breathing disturbances at night** (Apple Health, recent Apple Watch); **weight and body fat**, with the app or scale that recorded them, if the user turns on "Smart scale"; **daily food intake** (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat) recorded by another app, with the name of that app when Health provides it, if the user turns on "Food intake". On the phone only, the Application also counts the sleep apnoea notifications Apple writes to Health, to ask a question; this count never leaves the phone. On Android, the Application also asks for access to **history** (without it, Health Connect limits reading to the last 30 days) and **background** reading (nights and vitals updated without opening the Application).

**What is written**: only the period dates entered in the Application, if the user has turned on cycle tracking. The iPhone and Android Application writes no meals, weight or workouts to Health. If the user uses the Ecleptic app for Apple Watch, it saves to Apple Health the workouts tracked on the wrist (type, duration, heart rate, energy burned), like any workout app on the watch; the Application then reads them back like other workouts, without duplicates.

**Imported food intake.** It is only read for days when nothing is logged in Ecleptic, over the last 7 days. These totals are kept on Ecleptic's servers, for **365 days** at most, are included in the export and are never added to a meal logged in Ecleptic. They are used neither to calculate expenditure nor to calculate targets. Turning off the "Food intake" setting in the Application erases the totals already imported; withdrawing access only in the phone's settings stops reading, without erasing what has already been imported. If the user shares their nutrition with a coach, the coach sees these days (2.14).

**Weight and body fat read from Health.** Each weigh-in keeps its origin (entered by hand, Apple Health or Health Connect, and the original app or scale). A weigh-in entered by hand is never replaced by a value read from Health. Body fat measured by a scale is kept separate from the body scan estimates.

**Devices.** The "Devices" screen shows, for each app that writes to Health, the date of its last reading, type by type. This reading is calculated and kept on the phone only: it is neither sent to the server, nor written to Health, nor passed on to brands.

**Limited use.** Data read from Health Connect or Apple Health:

- is used **only** for the features visible in the Application: history of nights and workouts, vitals, Readiness Score, biological age, recovery, calorie targets, cycle tracking, the Lab, Eos (AI assistant), if the user turns it on, the Society (2.9), for the sports data they choose to share only, and, if they link to a coach, the coach space (2.14), for the categories they choose to share with that coach only;
- is **never sold**, nor transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers;
- is **never used for advertising**, personalised or not;
- is never used to assess creditworthiness or for lending, nor with a product or service that may qualify as a medical device, nor in any processing of protected health information within the meaning of the US HIPAA law;
- is only transferred to a third party with the user's **explicit consent**, for security purposes, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition the user would be informed of. The technical providers in article 4 process it on the publisher's behalf, with no right to use it for themselves. In particular, when the user requests a report or asks **Eos** (the AI assistant) a question, a summary of their measurements and, if their "AI analysis" consent is in its version of 15 November 2026 or later, their physiological situation (see 2.13), without name, email or identifier, are sent to the AI providers in article 4 (Google, Anthropic): this, like the photos sent by meal and label scanning, only happens after the "AI analysis" consent, asked on a dedicated screen before first use (what is sent, to whom, what never is) and withdrawable at any time in My profile › Data and privacy;
- is **read by no human**, except with the user's explicit consent (for example to handle a support request they send), for security purposes, to comply with the law, or in aggregated and anonymous form for internal operations.

**Withdrawal.** The user can withdraw the Application's access at any time in Health Connect (or in Apple Health): reading stops immediately. Data already imported stays in their Account until they delete it or delete their Account (article 6); they can also ask for it to be erased through the contact in article 1.

### 2.8. Origin, place and zones of workouts

This information is first of all for the user themselves. It is shown to nobody else as long as they have not turned on the Society (2.9).

- **Origin** of a workout imported from Apple Health or Health Connect: the application that recorded it (for example Strava, Garmin Connect, Nike Run Club), the device (watch or phone) and whether it was entered by hand. This information avoids duplicates and sets a **level of proof** (declared, phone, watch).
- **Place** of the workout (gym, pool, stadium, club…), recognised from the end-of-session location, a check-in or the place's QR code. The location is sent to the server for this search, then it is **not kept**: only the recognised place is.
- **Zones** crossed by an outdoor workout: municipality, district or large public space (for example "Quais de Seine, rive gauche"), with the kilometres and time spent in each. The calculation is done **on the phone**: the GPS track does not leave the device for this calculation.
- **Efforts**: best performances per session (estimated 1RM per exercise, load per machine, best running and swimming times), calculated from workouts.
- **Strava archive**, if the user imports the file they downloaded themselves from Strava: the file is read on the phone. Only workout summaries (sport, date, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate, calories, elevation) are saved, never tracks, photos, comments or followers. These workouts stay "declared": they do not enter any board.

### 2.9. The Society (social network)

The **Society** is the Application's social space. It is **optional** and **off by default**: nothing is shared with other users until the user has turned it on themselves. Once it is on, each type of data has its own setting (Settings › Social network): "No one", "Partners" or "Everyone", the latter meaning Society members, never the public on the Internet.

- **Profile**: nickname, generated avatar (an initial and a colour), visibility settings, anonymous mode, age group (adult, minor or unknown) calculated from the date of birth, status (Legend, Founder, league division, month of arrival), featured specialities and records, figures for the year and the last 4 weeks.
- **Profile photo** (optional): cropped to a 512-pixel square and compressed on the phone, stored in a private space with no public address. Visible to partners by default.
- **Partners and feed**: partner code and QR code, requests, partnerships, reactions. In the feed and on the profile, a workout shows the sport, the day (without the time), the duration, the distance, the figures specific to its sport (elevation gain, score and result of a match, ski runs, routes and grades in climbing, score of a WOD or a Hyrox, number of sets), in strength training the exercises of the workout and the muscles they work, without the loads, the place or zone if places are visible, records and reactions. These figures are only shared with the consent given on this version; heart rate, calories, how the session felt and loads are part of them only if the user has chosen so ("Workout details" item). To choose the 3D body on a workout's page (male or female), their partners receive the sex stated in their profile, without it ever being written on screen; "not specified" sends nothing. The "Don't show this session on my public profile" box removes it from the profile and the feed; it still counts in challenges, the league and boards, which only show totals.
- **Track, photos and note of a workout** (optional, chosen workout by workout): at the end of a workout or from its page, the user can show their partners:
  - a simplified copy of the track: 200 points at most, without time, speed or altitude, with the first 200 and last 200 metres removed; a route shorter than 600 metres is never shared. This copy is prepared on the phone. For a workout imported from Apple Health or Health Connect, the full track stays on the phone: only this copy is sent, and only if the user asks for it;
  - up to **4 photos**, reduced and compressed on the phone, with their hidden information (place and date of the shot, device) removed before sending. They are stored in a private space, with no public address;
  - a note of 140 characters at most.

  Nothing is ticked by default the first time. The Application then remembers the last choice, which the user sees and can change before each share. These items are visible to **their partners only**, in the feed, whatever the "Workouts" setting: they appear neither on the profile, nor in a ranking, nor on a gym screen, nor to a coach. The user can remove them at any time from the workout's page. They stop being visible and are erased **30 days** after the workout (article 6). When a partner looks at a track, the map tiles are loaded from their phone's map provider (article 4), which receives the area displayed and their IP address, with no account identifier. Minors cannot post any of this (article 10).
- **Workout details** (optional, reserved for adults, "No one" by default): if the user chooses "Partners" for the "My session details" setting, their partners who open one of their workouts from the last 30 days see its page: the **time** of the workout, the **heart rate** (average, maximum, minimum, curve and zones calculated with their reference maximum heart rate), the **calories**, **how the session felt**, the **sets and loads** of strength training, the **tonnage**, the **steps**, the swimming **lengths**, the **pace** and the details specific to the sport (WOD, Hyrox, ski, climbing, match). The page also shows a sentence: a record broken, the equivalent of the tonnage, or a strength rank among people of the same weight. The tonnage and this rank are calculated on their phone with the weight in their profile, then only their results (for example "8.4 t", "top 10 % on the squat") are saved with the workout; the weight is **never** sent to their partners. Blood oxygen, breathing, temperature, electrocardiogram and heart rate variability are **never** shown. The track stays limited to the simplified copy described above. Switching back to "No one" removes these details from their view straight away; the setting is saved with the version of the text accepted. Minors cannot choose "Partners" (article 10).
- **Published meals** (optional, chosen meal by meal, reserved for adults): when saving a scanned meal, the user can tick "Post my meal to Society". Their partners then see, in the feed: the **photo of the meal** (the scan photo, reduced and compressed on the phone, with its hidden information removed before sending, stored in a private space with no public address), the **type of meal** (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), its **day** (without the time) and the **names of the foods**. Calories, quantities, nutrient intakes and the time are **never** published. The box stays unticked until the user ticks it; the Application then remembers their last choice, which they see and can change before each save. Agreement to this version is requested at the first publication. A meal can reveal a diet: the user only publishes what they choose, and can remove a meal from the feed at any time; deleting the meal also removes its publication. Their partners can react to it.
- **Level points for photos and published meals**: a shared workout photo or a published meal earns level points, fewer and fewer as the day goes on (2, then 1, then 0.5), for a workout or meal from the same day or the day before. For this calculation, the Application keeps one row per photo or published meal: the day and the identifier of the workout or meal, without the photo. These points have no market value; they are withdrawn if the photo or meal is removed.
- **Challenges**: duels, Expeditions (groups of 3 to 6) and public challenges, including the Défi de l'Optimisateur: participation, progress and result. Joining a public challenge shows the nickname on its board, except in anonymous mode.
- **Place and spot boards**, place by place and on agreement when entering: values, rank and month of each record, never the time, the day or the session. Crowns, Founders, frozen monthly seasons and the hall of fame derive from them. Some boards are split by category (women, men, all), based on the sex entered in the profile.
- **Witnesses**: an adult partner present at the same place can confirm a performance; each then sees the other's request.
- **Proof video** (optional): 15 seconds at most, chosen by the user from their photo library, stored in a private space. Only the author and the place's reviewers see it (Ecleptic team, approved manager, Founders of the place who are still members).
- **Weekly league**, on sign-up and for adults: effort points, visible to the other members of the cohort of 30.
- **City Cup**: monthly ranking of cities and places, in totals only, with no member's name. A member's active days at their places contribute to it.
- **Racket**: declared matches (sport, opponent, score, optional place), confirmed by the opponent within 72 hours, and a calculated Elo rating.
- **Weight class**: the month's class (IPF classes), calculated from the weight on the 1st of the month. Only the class is kept and shared, never the weight, and it is visible to no one by default.
- **Suggestions**: a missing place (name, type, city and location taken on site) or a spot (name, sports, 40-metre corridor around a route shortened by its first and last 500 metres and simplified on the phone). A suggestion stays linked to the account and visible to the team. Once accepted, the place or spot is public, with no link to its author.
- **Team**: team name, code, weekly goal, members and administrator role. Members see each other's nickname, avatar and number of workouts for the week, never the content of those workouts. A workout hidden from the profile does not count.
- **Coach**: on a separate agreement, unticked by default and reserved for adults, the user links to a coach approved by Ecleptic. What the coach sees and what they can do are described in article 2.14.
- **Gym screen**: on a separate agreement, unticked by default, given place by place and reserved for adults, the nickname and the month's active days may appear on a gym's screen, visible to anyone present, with no photo or time, and never in anonymous mode.
- **Place managers**: management request (account, place, date, decision), verified by the Ecleptic team.
- **"Your year" review**: calculated for the user alone; they choose whether or not to share its card.
- **Society safety**: reports sent, blocked accounts, moderation decisions concerning them and their reasons, and anti-abuse counters (number of actions per day).

**What is never shown to other users**: the full GPS track, the start and finish of a route, real-time location, heart rate (except in the workout details the user chooses to show their partners), exact weight, exact age, sleep, nutrition and body composition data, except to the coach the user has linked to, for the categories they share with that coach only (2.14). The only exception, at the user's initiative: for a meal they choose to publish, the photo, the type, the day and the names of the foods, never the calories, quantities or nutrient intakes.

**Turning it off and withdrawing consent.** The user can turn off the Society at any time (Settings › Social network). Their profile, photos (profile and workout photos), shared tracks, notes, published meals and videos immediately stop being visible to other members; they leave their teams; their link with their coach is deleted along with their Society data if they do not turn the Society back on within 30 days (they can end it sooner from the "Coach" page); their ranking lines disappear at the nightly update; their partners and the participants in their ongoing challenges only see their nickname, marked inactive. If they do not turn the Society back on within **30 days**, their Society-specific data is erased automatically. They can ask for it to be erased immediately through the contact in article 1, or delete their Account. Blocks placed on them by other members and moderation decisions follow their own retention periods (article 6). Leaving a board removes their lines at once, seasons included.

### 2.10. Push notifications

If the user has turned on the Society and allowed notifications on their phone, the Application saves the device's **notification token** (a technical identifier provided by Apple or Google), the platform, the time zone, the language and their notification settings. The server then prepares the notifications that originate with another member (reaction, partnership request, challenge invitation, witness, video to review, match to confirm, crown taken back, place or spot opened): 3 per day at most, delayed between 10 pm and 8 am. They contain the other member's nickname or the place's name and the nature of the event, never a health figure. The token is removed at sign-out and when the user turns off the Society. The "Social network (Society)" switch in Settings › Notifications turns them off at any time.

### 2.11. Messaging

When the user uses messaging: content of messages (text, workout card they choose to share), the message they reply to, date sent and date edited, conversation, read status, mute setting. Other members never receive their account identifier. When a message is edited, its earlier version is not kept, except for the copy attached to a report already sent.

Each message goes through an automatic banned-words filter when it is sent, which keeps nothing. The Ecleptic team only reads a message if a member of the conversation reports it.

A message notification shows the author's nickname and the first 180 characters of the text. This text is not kept in the sending queue: it is read at the moment of sending, then passed to Apple or Google, which deliver it to the recipient's phone (article 4). It is not end-to-end encrypted, and it contains what the member wrote, including any health information. A workout card never appears in it, and a removed message is not passed on. When several messages arrive together, the notification only shows how many. On iPhone, the lock screen follows the phone's "Show Previews" setting; on Android, the text is hidden there.

Message notifications do not count towards the limit of 3 per day in article 2.10 and are not delayed at night. The "Messages" switch in Settings › Notifications turns them off at any time, and muting turns them off conversation by conversation.

### 2.12. Voice dictation

When the user taps the microphone in Eos's input bar, the Application turns their voice into text using the phone's speech recognition (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android). If the language is installed on the phone, transcription happens on the device and the voice does not leave it. Otherwise, the audio is sent to Apple's or Google's recognition service, which processes it under its own terms, as with keyboard dictation. The publisher never receives the recording, and the Application keeps neither the voice nor the audio. Only the resulting text is written into the input field: the user can edit it, and it is only sent when they send the message, like a written question to Eos (see 2.7 and article 4). Access to the microphone and to speech recognition can be withdrawn at any time in the phone's settings.

**Transcription of a voice message.** In a conversation with a coach, the Application can show the text of a voice message. This transcription happens on the phone only, with no fallback to an online service and no call to Ecleptic's servers or to an AI; the text is neither sent nor saved, and the audio file that passed through the phone's cache is erased.

### 2.13. Physiological situation

Some situations of the body or of daily rhythm distort the Application's calculations (expenditure, targets, readiness score, biological age, streaks) or call for different advice. The Application takes them into account in three ways, in order of reliability:

- **measured**: inferred from data that is already certain (weight and body composition, date of birth, weather and altitude). No question is asked;
- **declared**: the user ticks a box in "My situation" (high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes or prediabetes, sleep apnoea, a deficiency shown by a blood test, smoking, night or shift work, fasting, current injury or operation (area and return date optional) and, where relevant, taking a treatment), answers the question about periods at sign-up, or answers a one-line question (for example "Are you feeling ill?"). No answer is mandatory and "I'd rather not say" is always offered. Each answer is dated and kept in a log, which serves as proof and avoids asking the same question again. For fasting, only the time window of meals counts, never the reason;
- **detected**: the Application notices that a situation is likely (for example an unusual resting heart rate over several nights). Detection is used first to **ask the question**; on its own, it only applies risk-free corrections. Before having any effect, each detector first runs for at least two weeks "in the background": it computes and keeps its result without changing or showing anything, so that its reliability can be checked.

Examples of detections: the same painful area reported several times after sessions, meals within a short time window, nights tagged "nicotine", an Apple notification about breathing at night. Without an answer to a pain question, the Application only spares the area for two weeks (exercises replaced in workout templates); it never detects a religious period.

**Shift schedule.** If the user declares night or shift work, they can enter their shifts (optional): the hours of each shift and a repeating cycle, or a typical week. They are only used to time the bedtime reminder, the morning reminder and the energy gauge. They stay private, are included in the data export and can be erased at any time from the "My shifts" screen or by withdrawing "My situation" consent.

**Measurements.** The "Measurements" sheet shows blood pressure, blood glucose and breathing disturbances read from Health, with the markers published by health authorities (HAS, WHO). It never classifies the person and does not replace a health professional's advice. These measurements stay private: they are neither shown to other members nor shared with a coach.

The Application never names a situation the user has not declared, and uses the user's own words. These are **estimated analyses**: never a diagnosis, medical advice or the detection of a disease.

**Adults only.** The "My situation" questions and the detection of a health state (illness, body fat, age) are reserved for users aged 18 and over; an unknown date of birth closes them. Heat and altitude, which describe the context rather than the person, apply to everyone.

**Sharing.** This information is never sold, used for advertising or shown to other members. It is passed on to a coach only if the user decides so, situation by situation (see "Exchange with a coach" below). If the user has accepted "AI analysis" in its version of 15 November 2026 or a later one, Eos and the report receive the user's situation to adapt their advice: declared situations in the user's own words; measured or detected situations only as a pointer (for example "favour low-impact workouts"), without naming them. Acceptance of an earlier version remains valid for meal scanning and for Eos, but does not trigger this transfer.

**Exchange with a coach.** A user linked to a coach (2.14) can, from the "Coach" page of the Application:

- **share their situation with their coach**: they choose, one by one, the situations they have declared themselves and want to show their coach. A measured or detected situation cannot be shared. The coach sees the name of the situation, the area of an injury and the date of sharing, nothing else: not the treatment, the measurements or the detections. A first share requires a separate agreement, dated and kept as proof. The user can stop sharing at any time: it disappears from the coach's side at once. A situation that ends is no longer shown;
- **answer a suggestion from their coach**: a coach can note for their student a situation the student told them about (among high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes or prediabetes, sleep apnoea, low iron, vitamin D or vitamin B12, smoking, night or shift work, fasting, current injury or surgery; never periods or menopause). This suggestion changes nothing in the calculations. The user confirms it, corrects it or says it is not right. What they confirm is recorded as their own declaration, with the same dated proof as in "My situation", and shared with that coach. What they decline is erased: the coach only learns that the suggestion was not kept, without knowing whether it was declined or left unanswered. A suggestion left unanswered is erased after **30 days**;
- **fill in or confirm their details**: injuries, sports background, preferences, equipment and availability. The coach can enter them; the user sees them, confirms them, corrects them or says they are not right. The user can also fill them in themselves. The coach's personal notes are not shown to the user, who can ask the coach, responsible for them, to disclose them.

Sharing a situation and a coach's suggestions are reserved for users aged **18 or over**. A coach is not a health professional: they undertake to note only what their student told them, and to give no medical advice. Shared situations and the coach's suggestions are erased when the user leaves their coach or withdraws the "My situation" agreement. Details (injuries included) and the coach's personal notes are erased 30 days after the link ends, unless the user is coached by this coach again by then; the user can ask their coach, or contact@ecleptic.app, to erase their details sooner.

**Withdrawal.** In My profile › Data and privacy › "My situation", "Withdraw my consent and erase everything" erases at once the answers, their log, all situations (declared, measured or detected) and detector results, and stops any new detection for the account until consent is given again. Proof of the withdrawal (date, version of the text) is kept with the other proofs of consent.

### 2.14. Coach

A user can link to a coach approved by Ecleptic. The link is **optional**, given through a separate agreement, unticked by default, reserved for people aged **18 or over**, and requires the Society to be turned on (2.9). The coach's access stops as soon as the user withdraws their agreement or ends the link from the "Coach" page. A coach is not a health professional: they undertake to give no medical advice.

**Who is responsible.** For the user's account and what they choose to share, Ecleptic is the data controller. For what the coach decides and manages themselves (files, notes, forms, documents, invoices, sales, promo codes, prospects, classes, appointment requests, emails they write, tools they connect), the coach is the data controller and Ecleptic acts on their behalf, as a processor, under the data processing agreement annexed to the coaches' terms of sale. A request about this data can be sent to the coach, or to contact@ecleptic.app, which passes it on.

**What the coach always sees: workouts.** All the user's workouts, including those hidden from the public profile: date and time, type, duration, distance, perceived effort, title, exercises, sets, loads, calories and timed blocks, as well as their profile photo. Never tracks or places; heart rate only with the "Watch" box.

**What the user chooses to share in addition.** In "What my coach sees", boxes unticked by default, which can be changed at any time:

- **Goal and file**;
- **Body and age**: weight, measurements, body fat, biological age; for weigh-ins and body fat read from Health, the original app or scale (2.7), over one year;
- **Recovery**: Readiness, sleep;
- **Nutrition**: the meal journal (below), with sex and age band to compare intake with reference values;
- **Watch**: resting heart rate and workout heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, steps, active calories; never oxygen saturation, breathing or temperature;
- **Progress photos**.

Never body scan photos. Nothing is shared, apart from workouts, before the user chooses. The coach can ask for a category; the user accepts all, part or none of it. After a refusal, the coach can only ask for the same thing again 7 days later; a request left unanswered expires after 14 days, without counting as a refusal. Photos (progress and meal photos), the health questionnaire and the "Connected tools" agreement can never be requested. Each choice is kept as proof, with the version of the text displayed. Unticking a box removes access at once: the coach reads this data on Ecleptic's servers, each time they open it.

**Meal journal.** If the user ticks "Nutrition" under its current text, their coach reads their meals one by one, day by day, up to one year back: for each meal, the time of day (breakfast, snack, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner), the time, the title the user gave it, its foods and their quantities, its calories and macronutrients, and the name of scanned products; for each day, the total against the calorie and protein target, with a note of a training day if the coach's target takes it into account.

- **Dated agreement**: ticking the box under this text counts as agreement to the detail of meals; Ecleptic keeps its date. A user who ticked "Nutrition" under an older text keeps sharing their intake and foods for the day, without a meal-by-meal breakdown, time or title, until they confirm the box again: the Application shows it to them marked "Updated". The coach can ask for this agreement in the conversation.
- **Meal titles**: this is free text that the user writes for themselves; it is only shown to the coach with this agreement.
- **Imported intake**: days imported from Health (2.7) appear marked "imported from" the original app: the day's total and macronutrients, never a food, time or photo.
- **Withdrawal**: unticking "Nutrition", including from an older version of the Application, immediately removes the journal, the meal details and the meal photos.
- The journal is not used in the weekly review written by an AI, which only reads the day's intake.

**Meal photos.** If the user ticks "My meal photos" and confirms, their coach sees, in the meal journal, the photo of each meal photographed, up to one year back, photos already taken included.

- **Conditions**: this agreement requires the "Nutrition" box and the agreement to the detail of meals. Withdrawing either one also withdraws the photos.
- **Separate, dated agreement**: it can only be given from this panel, never through "Share everything"; the coach cannot ask for it. Ecleptic keeps the date of the agreement and a record of each time it is given and withdrawn.
- **Display**: the photo is served only to the signed-in coach, in the Application or on their web console, through a temporary 10-minute link, renewed as long as the page stays open. No button allows it to be downloaded or shared. As with any image shown on screen, a screenshot remains technically possible, and the temporary link can be copied during its 10 minutes.
- **Display log**: each display of a photo by the coach (thumbnail included, and at each renewal of the link) is recorded: coach, meal, date and time. This log is included in the user's data export. Lines older than one year are erased every night; all of them are erased with the link.
- **Withdrawal**: unticking the panel withdraws the agreement straight away; no photo is served any more. A link already served stays valid for 10 minutes at most.
- **Never AI, never third parties**: the photos do not leave Ecleptic's storage; none is used in the review written by an AI.

**The coach's target, meal plans and tasks.**

- **Calorie target**: the coach can suggest a target, never below a floor calculated by Ecleptic from the profile; the suggestion reveals neither the weight nor the BMI to them. They can plan a difference for training days (0 to 600 kcal, on days when a session from the coach is planned or done). If the user accepts, the target replaces Ecleptic's until they switch back or the link ends.
- **"Stable weight" rule**: if the coach turns it on and the user shares "Body and age", Ecleptic looks every night at their weigh-ins over the last 14 days. When the weight barely moves any more (at least 4 weigh-ins, less than 0.2 kg apart), Ecleptic suggests, in the coach's name, a new target 100 kcal higher or lower. The user accepts or declines it; nothing applies by itself. Never below the floor, one suggestion every 14 days at most.
- **Meal plans, recipes and supplements**: meals planned per day, instructions, recipes, supplements suggested from Ecleptic's catalogue (dose below the ANSES ceiling). The user notes "I ate this", "Something else" or "Swap" (one of the alternatives planned by the coach, or an Ecleptic recipe for the same meal): the coach sees these answers and the title and calories of the meal chosen. A meal noted this way enters the user's journal as a manual entry. A suggested supplement is only added if the user chooses it.
- **Tasks**: title, instruction, planned days and what the user ticks (done, or a value); a reminder, if they choose one, is scheduled on their phone only.
- **Hydration plan**: if the user ticks “Nutrition”, their coach reads the water goal they chose in the Water Cycle and can suggest a plan: a daily goal (1 to 4 litres), an extra amount on training days, a number of reminders (0 to 6 per day) and, if the coach plans it, an evening check. The user accepts or declines; nothing changes without their agreement, and they can go back to their own setting whenever they like. The water recommendation calculated by Ecleptic from the weight is shown to the coach only if the user shares “Body and age”. The Water Cycle estimates the water drunk, it does not measure it: the coach reads no volume drunk, only the evening answer (“goal reached”, yes or no) when the plan asks for it.
- **Reminders set by the coach**: once the hydration plan is accepted, the coach sets the number of water reminders. They can also turn off the morning score reminder, the bedtime reminder and the body scan reminder, or space out the weigh-in reminder. They can never turn back on a reminder the user turned off, nor add one of another kind: the user's settings and those of their phone have the last word. So that the coach sees what is actually on, the Application tells them, for each family of reminders, whether the user leaves them on (yes or no), with no other detail. These reminders remain scheduled on the user's phone: the coach's setting applies the next time the Application is opened.
- **“Too many reminders?”**: the user can tell their coach that their reminders are too many or badly timed (one reason out of three, once a day at most). The coach reads it on the user's file.
- Water settings and the state of reminders are sent to Ecleptic's servers only for a user linked to a coach; without a coach, they stay on the phone.
- This is not medical follow-up: the coach undertakes to make no plan for an illness, an eating disorder or a pregnancy, and to refer to a health professional.

**Weekly review written by an AI.** If the user ticks, in "What my coach sees", the box "My coach can have an AI write a review of my week from the data I share with them", their coach can receive a draft review every week. This box is separate from the others: it is never ticked by "Share everything"; the coach can ask for it, but only the user ticks it. It also requires the "AI analysis" consent and health consent.

- **What is calculated**: the review's figures are calculated by Ecleptic, not by the AI: sessions planned and done, best set and progress per exercise, records, volume, progress towards goals; then, only for the categories the user already shares with their coach: average and regularity of sleep, intake against target, weight. Added to these are their closed answers (yes or no, choice, rating) to the check-in their coach sends them; never a free-text answer.
- **What is sent to the AI provider** (article 4): these figures, the user's first name, their language and the tone instruction written by the coach. Nothing else: no email address, identifier, photo or message.
- **What never goes into a review**: the user's situation (2.13), temperature, breathing, oxygen saturation, watch data, photos, appointments, and any category the user does not share with their coach.
- **What the coach does**: they read the draft, can edit it, send it or ignore it. The user can also agree, if their coach chooses this option, to receive it without the coach reading it first. The review arrives in the conversation with the coach and carries the note "Written with the help of AI". It contains no diagnosis, medical advice or dose.
- **Withdrawal**: unticking the box stops the writing and erases drafts not sent. Withdrawing health consent or the "AI analysis" consent has the same effect. Reviews already received stay in the conversation.

**Situation and details.** See "Exchange with a coach" (2.13).

**Health questionnaire, forms and documents.**

- **Health questionnaire**: sent by the coach, optional, on a separate agreement. The coach sees the result and the signature. The answers stay on the phone, unless the user ticks a separate box; unticking it, which they can do at any time, erases them from the server.
- **Forms and documents**: answers, signature (name typed, date, version, fingerprint) and files, visible to the coach and the user. The coach can keep a library of documents in Ecleptic (PDFs, images, 20 MB per file) and send them to their students; a student only reads what is meant for them, during their coaching, through a temporary link. A document removed by the coach disappears from conversations ("Document removed") and its file is erased by the following night at the latest.

**Messages, voice messages, groups and videos.**

- **Messages with the coach**: text, quoted reply, editing for 24 hours, reactions (one emoji out of six), voice messages, photos and documents. The notification of a message from the coach shows their nickname and the first 180 characters of the text, as for messaging (2.11); a voice message, photo or document never appears in it, only its type is shown.
- **Voice messages** (2 minutes at most) and **photos**: stored in a private space, read through a temporary link, only by the user and their coach, or by the members of the group. A photo is re-saved on the phone before sending: its location and metadata are removed. No analysis by artificial intelligence.
- **User's first name**: when the coach prepares a message, a template, an email or a text message for a student, the Application can write the first name from that student's profile into it (40 characters at most); the coach then sees this first name in the preview, and the message sent carries it. This is never the case in a group, and a profile without a first name gives none.
- **Scheduled and automatic messages**: the coach can write a message that goes out later, to the user alone or to a group; nobody sees the other recipients of a message sent to several students. They can also create up to 5 rules that send a message on their behalf to a student who has gone several days without a workout (3 to 30 days, counted from the last workout). Ecleptic keeps a log of these messages for 90 days (rule, student, message, date). No text leaves Ecleptic and no AI is involved.
- **Automatic emails**: if a message from the coach stays unread for 48 hours, an email points it out (once a week at most); the coach can receive a summary of their unread messages (once a day at most). They go to the account's address, which the coach never sees, and only contain nicknames, group names and numbers, never the text of a message. A link in each email turns them off without signing in, and so does Settings › Emails.
- **Coach's groups**: by invitation, the user chooses to join. Other members see their nickname, avatar and messages in the group, never their file or data. They can leave at any time; they leave all groups when the link ends. Declining an invitation is not shown to the coach.
- **Group challenge**: an adult member can join the challenge the coach launches in a group. Only the participants and the coach see the leaderboard, with each person's first name (or their nickname if there is none) and figure; a member who has not joined does not appear in it. Nothing appears in the Society.
- **Technique video**: a video of 60 seconds at most that the user films or chooses and sends to their coach, with, if they wish, a note, the name of the exercise and the workout. It shows their body, sometimes their face, their voice and the place: the user is asked to film themselves without any other recognisable person. Only the user and their coach see it, for 3 days after sending; it is then erased from our servers. The coach can save it to their device before then: the copy they keep is then their responsibility. Ecleptic records whether the coach has opened it. No video is analysed by an AI or shown in a notification.
- **Coach's exercise videos**: the coach can attach a demonstration video (60 seconds at most) to their exercises, which their students see during their coaching, through a temporary link, with no download button.

**To reach the user outside the Application.** In "What my coach sees", "Outside Ecleptic" section:

- **Phone number** (optional): the user enters it themselves, for this coach only. The coach is the only person who sees it; they can use it to send the user a text message from their own phone, with their own number. Ecleptic sends no text messages, keeps neither the text nor any record of a text message, and passes the number on to no one else: it never leaves through the tools the coach connects. Ecleptic keeps the date on which the number was given. The user can change or remove it at any time; it is erased at once, and in any case when the coaching ends, whatever the reason. A copy the coach may have noted elsewhere is their sole responsibility. The number is included in the export.
- **Emails from the coach**: the coach can email the user from their file, in the Application or on their console. Ecleptic sends the email to the user's account address, which the coach does not see, in the coach's name ("Name via Ecleptic"). If the user replies, their reply leaves from their own mailbox: the coach then sees their address. The text is written by the coach, who is responsible for it and undertakes to write only about the coaching and their own services, with no health data. No attachments or buttons. At most 3 emails to the same student over 7 days, and 20 emails in total per coach over 24 hours. These emails are received by default. The footer of each email says why the user receives it and lets them stop receiving emails from this coach, in one click and without signing in; the "Emails from" switch for this coach, in the Application, does the same. To respect this choice, Ecleptic keeps a fingerprint of the address (never the address itself), specific to this coach. If the user changes their account's email address, their opt-out is carried over to the new address. Ecleptic also keeps, for 12 months, a log of each email sent: the date, the status and the template used; never the subject, the text or the address.

**Tools connected by the coach.** A coach can connect their own tools to their space: a spreadsheet, management or gym software, or Zapier. They do so with an **API key**, which lets their tool read some of their management data, or with a **webhook address**, to which Ecleptic sends a short message when an event happens. These tools are chosen by the coach, who is responsible for them; they are not Ecleptic's providers. No health data leaves this way.

- **What can leave without the user's agreement** (data the coach manages themselves, closed lists): for a student, a code specific to this coach (never the account identifier), the nickname under which the coach sees them, the coaching status and its start and end dates; for a scheduled session, its kind (training or appointment), its title written by the coach, its time, its planned duration and whether it is cancelled; for a sale or a refund, the package, the amount, the currency, the date and the invoice number; for a class booking, the class, the student, or only the note "participant without an account"; for a signed form, its kind, its title and the signing date, never the answers (the health questionnaire is not included: see below). Prospects and the coach's personal slots are not included.
- **What leaves only with the "Connected tools" agreement**: "session completed" (the title of the session sent by the coach, its date, the student), "check-in filled in" (the date, the student, never the answers), "health questionnaire signed" (the kind and date, the student, never the title or the answers), and the note "done" or "missed" for a scheduled session. No figures: no actual duration, effort, load or set.
- **What never leaves**: weight, measurements, photos, sleep, Readiness, situation, watch data, meals, answers to forms and check-ins, file, notes, messages, email address, phone, date of birth, age, sex, account identifier.
- **The agreement** is given in "What my coach sees", in the "Tools connected by" box for this coach, after a sheet that says what is sent; it cannot be given during a trial. Ecleptic keeps its date and a record of each time it is given and withdrawn. Unticking the box cancels sends still pending; what has already gone stays with the coach's tool, which is answerable for it. The agreement lapses when the coaching ends and is included in the export.
- **Security**: the key is shown to the coach only once; Ecleptic only keeps a fingerprint of it. 5 active keys and 3 addresses at most per coach; 60 calls per minute and 5,000 per day. Each call is logged (key, route, response, duration, fingerprint of the caller's IP address). Webhook addresses are checked at each send, and messages are signed; an address that fails 20 times in a row is switched off. When the coach's subscription ends, the tool can still read, but nothing more is sent.

**Invoices, purchases and rewards.**

- **Coach's invoices**: name, address if given, service, amount and payment, visible to the coach and to the user they are sent to. When the coach prepares an invoice, the Application suggests the user's name: the one on their last invoice, otherwise the name entered at their last card payment, otherwise their first name; the coach can write it differently. The user cannot have an invoice erased during the legal retention period, even by deleting their account.
- **Purchases from the coach**: the package bought, its price, prepaid sessions and their use, payments and refunds, a request to start before the end of the cooling-off period, a withdrawal or termination statement (surname, first name, email for the acknowledgement), and, for a purchase made on the public page before having an account, the buyer's first name and email. An invoice is issued for each payment. A buyer without an account activates their purchase in the Application with a code received by email: they then accept the Terms of Use and confirm that they are 15 or over; the activation link is valid for 30 days.
- **Promo codes**: when a student or a buyer uses one of the coach's discount codes, Ecleptic keeps the code, the package, the price, the discount and the price paid, with the student's nickname or the buyer's first name and email. The coach sees the uses with this nickname or first name, never the buyer's email. A code can only be used once per person (account or email address). Uses are kept like the invoices that carry the discount: 11 years after the purchase's last invoice or payment (3 years if there is neither). Attempts with wrong codes are counted, with a fingerprint of the IP address on the public page, and kept for 2 days. For a subscription, the discount is applied by Stripe, on the coach's account.
- **Referral by students**: if the coach opens it, the user receives a link to their coach's public page. This link carries a token that does not say who they are: the page only says "recommended by a student". The coach, on the other hand, sees which student sent each prospect. When the person sent becomes a student and then pays for a package, the coach decides whether or not to give the student who sent them 1 to 5 sessions. Ecleptic keeps the referral (referrer, person referred, status, dates, sessions given). The referrer only sees numbers. A free session is valid for 3 months; it is not a payment: no invoice, no fees.

**People without an account: public page, prospects, appointments and classes.**

- **Coach's public page**: their texts, city, places of practice, links, articles and their images are public when they open their page. They are only found by search engines if the coach asks for it. The preview of a shared link is prepared by the console's server, with no cookies or visitor log. The calculation tools on the page run in the visitor's browser: no figure is sent or kept.
- **Coaching requests (prospects)**: a person without an account can write to a coach from their public page: first name, contact, goal, message. The coach is responsible for it. A fingerprint of the IP address, never the address itself, is used to limit abuse. The form is reserved for people aged 18 or over and asks them not to write any medical information.
- **Coach's emails to a prospect**: when a coach writes from the console to a person who contacted them, or who gave them their agreement, Ecleptic sends the email for them: the address, the first name, the coach's text and the date sent. Each email says why the person receives it and lets them stop receiving emails from this coach, in one click; Ecleptic then keeps a fingerprint of the address so as to send nothing more.
- **Appointment requests**: when a coach turns them on, their public page shows their free slots for the next 14 days. A person without an account can choose a slot and send a request with their first name, their email address and, if they wish, a note of 280 characters at most; they declare that they are 18 or over and that they will write nothing about their health. Ecleptic also keeps the slot, the language of the page, the version of the policy displayed and a fingerprint of their IP address (never the address itself). The coach receives the first name, the email, the note and the slot, through a notification that does not carry the person's name; the person joins their prospects. The coach accepts or declines within 48 hours, and at the latest before the time of the slot; without an answer, the request is cancelled. Ecleptic sends the person, on the coach's behalf and in the language of the page, an acknowledgement of receipt, then the acceptance (with a file to add to their calendar), the refusal, the cancellation or the change of time, duration or place (with the updated calendar file). A tracking link, secret and never shown to the coach, shows the first name, the date, the time, the coach's name and the place once the appointment is accepted, and lets the person cancel the request, then the appointment until it starts; the coach is told of the cancellation. Limits: 5 requests per hour from the same IP address, 20 per day for the same coach, a single pending request per address and per coach; a note rejected by the content filter is not passed on. If the coach has connected their Google calendar with write access (2.15), the appointment is written there under the title "Personal slot" only, with no first name or place.
- **Group classes**: to book without an account, the participant gives their first name and email; these are used for the confirmation, the reminder the day before, the video call link one hour before, cancellation and the coach's message to those booked, nothing else. The coach sees the first name, not the email (except in their export). A student who books from the Application is seen by their nickname.

**The coach themselves.** For a user registered as a coach, Ecleptic also processes: their subscription (tier, status, dates, Stripe or in-app purchase identifiers) and the 1% service fee they pay on their sales, with their invoices and credit notes; their company name, SIRET number and billing address; their logo and cover photo (visible to their students, to visitors to their page and, for the logo, on their invoices); referrals between coaches and the Zenith discount (the coach never learns which student triggered it: Ecleptic only keeps a fingerprint); their API keys and webhook addresses; the summary of their week sent every Monday by email (on by default, turned off in one click or in their settings; it only contains numbers and students' nicknames); the calendars they connect (2.15). If they create or join a team, see 2.16.

**Trial.** A 14-day trial with a coach is given on a separate agreement, with sessions and messages from the coach, some of them scheduled, one a day at most.

### 2.15. Calendars connected by a coach

A coach can connect their other calendars to their Ecleptic calendar, in two ways:

- **through a calendar address** (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or other): the coach pastes their calendar's secret address. Ecleptic keeps it encrypted and reads it again every 10 minutes;
- **through a direct link with Google Calendar**: the coach signs in to their Google account and agrees that the Application may see the list of their calendars and read and write their events and, if they choose, that it may read and check off their Google Tasks. The Application asks for no other access to the Google account.

What Ecleptic does with them:

- **Read**: for the calendars the coach has chosen, the Application reads the date, time, duration and title of each event, and whether it shows the coach as busy or free, over the past 14 days and the next 8 weeks. These events are shown in the coach's Ecleptic calendar and remove the matching slots from their bookable slots. Neither the description, the location nor the guests are kept. The title is only visible to the coach.
- **Link to a student**: the coach can link an event to one of their students. The student then sees an appointment with their coach at that time, without the title or the calendar's name, and receives the usual reminder.
- **Write** (direct link only, if the coach chooses a calendar to write to): their Ecleptic appointments and personal slots are created, moved and deleted in that Google calendar. The title written is "Appointment · @nickname" ("Appointment" if the nickname is not approved), with the place, for an appointment with a student, and "Personal slot", without a place, for a personal slot or an appointment request from the public page; never a visitor's first name, a coach's note or health data. No guests are added and Google notifies no one.
- **Edit or delete, at the coach's request** (direct link only): from Ecleptic, the coach can change the title, date, times and availability (busy or free) of an event in their Google calendars that they organise, or delete it (it goes to Google's bin). One occurrence of a series at a time. The description, location, video call and guests are never changed; Google notifies the guests only if the coach ticks the box provided.
- **Recognise their students**: the Application looks in an event's **title** (never its description or location) for the first name, the nickname or a name the coach saved themselves for one of their students, and may compare a hashed fingerprint of the email addresses of a Google event's guests with those of their students (guests' addresses are only read in passing, never kept). It then suggests an action to the coach (for example sending a workout) in the "New in your calendar" list: **nothing is sent to a student without the coach's click**. This recognition uses fixed rules, without artificial intelligence, on the coach's own calendars and only their students currently being coached; the names they save are used for them alone. The coach can turn it off, and review or delete these names, in their calendar settings.
- **Google Tasks** (if the coach allows it, through a separate Google agreement): the Application reads the title, due day, status and list name of their Google Tasks, open or completed within the last 7 days at most, and checks them off at their request. **Never their notes.**
- **What is never done**: this data is not sold, passed on to other third parties, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. It is not used in any review written by an AI. No one at Ecleptic reads it, except at the coach's request to solve a problem, for security, or where the law requires it.
- **Security**: the address of a calendar and access to the Google account are kept encrypted and are never shown, even to the coach.
- **Withdrawal**: removing an address, unticking a calendar or tapping "Unlink Google Calendar" erases from Ecleptic the address or the access, the imported events and their links to students. Unlinking also removes access at Google. Events Ecleptic has written to Google stay there; the coach deletes them themselves if they wish. The coach can also remove access from their Google account (myaccount.google.com, "Security", "Third-party apps & services"). Removing a calendar or unlinking Google also erases the items of the "New in your calendar" list that come from it, and unlinking erases the tasks read; turning off access to tasks erases them too. Turning recognition off stops the suggestions; the list already made follows its periods (article 6). The names the coach saved for a student are erased when that student's coaching ends, when the coach deletes them, or with their account.

Ecleptic's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

### 2.16. A coach's team

A coach can create a team for their business (gym, studio) and become its manager. They invite other coaches and assistants to it. Each coach keeps their own students: until the user gives the agreement below, neither the manager nor the assistants see their data, and the other coaches of the team never see them, not even in a list.

**Reading by my coach's team.** When the user's coach is part of a team, "What my coach sees" shows the box "Reading by" this coach's "team", unticked by default. Ticking it opens a sheet that says what the team reads and what it never does; only "Accept" gives the agreement. Ecleptic keeps its date and a record of each time it is given and withdrawn (version "c11-v1").

- **Who reads**: the team's manager and their assistants. An assistant is a person the manager invites to help them; they are not necessarily a coach and do not coach any student. Two assistants per coach at most.
- **What they read**: the user's file and trends as their coach sees them, with the same boxes (2.14): their workouts and their planned week and, depending on their boxes, their goal and file (including their details, 2.13), their body and age, their recovery and sleep, their nutrition (each day's meals with their foods and calories, against the target), their watch, and the measurements their coach created for them. A box unticked for the coach is unticked for the team too.
- **What they never see**: what the user does not share with their coach; the coach's private notes; messages, voice messages, photos (progress photos as well as meal photos), videos and documents; the time, the moment of the day and the title of meals; the situation shared with the coach (2.13); the health questionnaire and forms; the phone number and email address; purchases and invoices.
- **Read only**: they cannot change or send anything: no workout, no plan, no message, no change of coach.
- **Why**: so that the business can support the coach in following the user (advice, continuity, quality of coaching).
- **Reading log**: each reading of the file or of a trend by the manager or an assistant is recorded: who (nickname and role), when, and what was read. The user's coach sees the readings of their students, the manager those of the whole team, an assistant their own. The user also sees it in the Application, under the agreement ("Who has read your profile"): the reader's nickname and role, what was read, date and time, for the 20 most recent readings; the full log is included in their export (article 7). It is kept for 365 days. When the manager reads the file of a student they coach themselves, nothing is recorded.
- **End of the agreement**: unticking the box withdraws it at once. ### Collecting explicit consent for health data

On first use of the tracking features, the Application collects **explicit, specific and informed consent** (dedicated screen, separate positive action, clear information about the processing). It immediately stops all processing of health data: the Application no longer reads Apple Health or Health Connect, location or motion; it no longer records meals, workouts, nights or measurements and cancels scheduled reminders; the server no longer calculates anything (targets, scores, reports, Eos's answers) and no longer sends anything to the AI providers. The Application only shows a screen from which the user can give their consent again, export their data, delete their Account or sign out. Their data is kept for **30 days** without any use, to allow time to change their mind: if they do not give their consent again by then, their Account is deleted with all its data (article 6). Where a legal obligation requires certain data to be kept before deletion (a coach's cash journal), the Account stays paused, with no processing of health data, until that obligation is met. Society and the link with a coach remain governed by their own consents (2.9 and 2.14); nothing new is published there during the pause. Deleting the Account does not cancel the subscription, which is cancelled from the App Store or Google Play.

## Article 8 — Security

The data controller implements appropriate technical and organisational measures (art. 32 GDPR), including: encryption of data in transit (TLS) and at rest at the host, strict access control and authentication, separated environments, logging, minimisation of data sent to providers (including AI providers), regular backups. Data tables are protected by row-level access rules (each user only accesses their own data) and photos kept on our servers are stored in private spaces, partitioned per account; body scan photos stay on the user's phone. In the Society, any access to another member's data goes through a single server-side check, which applies their visibility settings and blocks; profile photos, workout photos and proof videos have no public address.

In the event of a data breach likely to result in a risk to people's rights and freedoms, the CNIL will be notified within 72 hours and, in the event of a high risk, the people concerned will be informed (art. 33 and 34 GDPR).

## Article 9 — Cookies and trackers

9.1. The Application uses trackers that are **strictly necessary** for it to work (authentication token, local preferences, security), exempt from consent, and an **audience measurement run by the publisher itself**: a random installation identifier stored on the device, used only to produce anonymous usage statistics, never cross-referenced with the account or other processing, kept for 13 months at most (identifier and events), and which the user can object to at any time in Settings › Privacy. These statistics are only shared with third parties in aggregated form, except for the transitional relay to PostHog described in article 4. This setup aims at the consent exemption provided by the CNIL for audience measurement. No advertising tracker is built in; no cross-app tracking (no App Tracking Transparency on iPhone; on Android, the advertising identifier is neither requested nor read).

9.2. If trackers subject to consent were added (for example a non-exempt audience measurement tool), prior consent would be collected through a dedicated interface offering, at the same level, the possibility to accept, refuse and configure, withdrawable at any time from the Settings, with the choice remaining valid for no more than 13 months.

## Article 10 — Minors

The Application is intended for people aged at least **16** (18 for the body composition scan and the conversation with Eos). In France, the age of digital consent is set at **15** (art. 45 of the French Data Protection Act): below that age, processing based on consent requires the joint consent of the minor and the holder of parental authority. If the data controller learns that an account was created in breach of these conditions, it will delete it along with the related data. The "My situation" questions and the detection of a health state are also reserved for users aged 18 and over (see 2.13).

In the Society, a 16- or 17-year-old user **never** appears in a public ranking: they are only visible to their partners, whom they add in person (QR code or link), and so is their photo. They cannot attach a track, a photo or a note to their workouts, and cannot publish meals. They cannot show the details of their workouts (2.9). The link with a coach, a coach group's challenge, the league, public challenges, the gym screen and place management are reserved for adults, and they only play racket with their partners. An unknown date of birth counts as being a minor.
